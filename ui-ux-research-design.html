Featured case study

CreativeBaseStation

An online creative community extending the Tech TurnUp classroom through discussion, sharing, and student identity-building.

CASE
001

Project overview

Reaching beyond the classroom into an online creative community.

CreativeBaseStation was proposed as an online extension of the Tech TurnUp classroom. Students could explore discussion boards with peers, create posts, and build a personal profile in a safe, engaging environment for exploration, play, and collaboration.

Connor Vu · Jasmine Voon · Marilyn Ortega

ScopeUser research, UI/UX design, desktop + mobile adaptation
ToolsFigma · Adobe Illustrator
Timeline3 days

01 — Understand

Start with
the people.

We interviewed Tech TurnUp students ages 10–17 and faculty members to understand engagement, belonging, and unmet needs.

01 / Listen

Rapid interviews

We asked where students felt engaged and excited, what they wanted more of, and what faculty needed from an online extension of the program.

02 / Synthesize

Affinity diagramming

Interview responses were grouped to identify recurring statements and patterns that could inform the platform’s services and features.

03 / Interpret

Research insights

Students wanted more time to create, engaged more with hands-on and computer-related projects, and used different technologies depending on age and situation.

“I wish there was more time dedicated to creating.”— Student interview insight

02 — Define

Design for
belonging.

The design direction translated the research into a community-oriented space that supports creativity, inclusion, and student agency.

01

Design accessibly

Prioritize high-contrast pairings for visual accessibility.

02

Present simply

Create a modern interface while ensuring minimal clutter.

03

Guide clearly

Use hierarchy and color theory to highlight key actions.

03 — Explore

From ideas
to interfaces.

Sprint sketches and wireframes explored how students could enter the community, discover conversations, and express their interests.

01

Welcome and exploration

A clear starting point for new and returning users, with obvious paths to create, explore, and share.

02

Discussion and sharing

A bulletin-style board for topic-based conversations, reactions, surveys, polls, and sharing creative projects.

03

Creator identity

A profile builder and interest web where students could select interests, build badges, and connect their projects.

04

Responsive experiences

Desktop and mobile adaptations considered how students might access the community across devices.

04 — Visual system

A voice that feels
bold + welcoming.

The visual direction was designed to feel empowering, inclusive, and energetic while keeping content legible and actions clear.

Fresh Mint#3FF2B4
Novel Night#1E1726
Iris Bloom#7046A5
New Page#FFFFFF

The design system also established a typographic hierarchy, logo variations, and approved color pairings informed by accessibility considerations.

05 — Deliver

CreativeBaseStation
takes shape.

The final concept brings the research and design principles together across a landing experience, conversation board, profile builder, and interest web.

01 / LANDING

Choose your adventure

Students can enter Explore, Share, or Create, while parents and new visitors can learn about the program through the newsletter.

02 / COMMUNITY

Explore and converse

Students can share projects, react with friendly phrases or stickers, and take part in polls and surveys.

03 / PROFILE

Build a creator identity

Interests, badges, posts, and friends help students represent themselves and discover connections with peers.
